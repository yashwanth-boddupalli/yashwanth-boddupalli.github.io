★ Featured project

LCO CONNECT

MULTI-TENANT SAAS · WEB APPLICATION

A platform concept for local cable TV, broadband and Wi-Fi operators — the people who still run on phone calls, WhatsApp and paper records. LCO CONNECT brings customers, payments, complaints, technicians and plans into one digital workspace. It grew out of thinking about a real local business, Tiranga Cable TV & Internet: from solving a real local problem to exploring a scalable SaaS solution.

LCO registration→Verification→Super Admin approval→LCO workspace→Customers→Payments→Service requests
  • Multi-tenant architecture — each operator gets its own workspace
  • Supabase auth, RLS policies, SQL triggers & user management
  • Customer onboarding, verification & service management
  • Broadband & cable plan billing with UPI payment exploration
  • Admin dashboard: customers, payments, complaints, requests
  • Technician dashboard: tickets, status updates, closures
